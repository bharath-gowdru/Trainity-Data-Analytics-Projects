# Trainity Data Analytics Projects

Seven data analytics projects completed during an online Data Analyst internship at Trainity (Jun 2024 - Sep 2024). Each folder holds the project presentation.

| # | Project | Business problem | Tools | Key findings |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Instagram User Analytics](01-instagram-user-analytics) | Marketing and investor metrics for a social platform | SQL (MySQL) | 26 inactive users, 13 flagged as likely bots; best days for ad launches are Thursday and Sunday |
| 2 | [Operations Analytics & Investigating Metric Spike](02-operations-analytics-metric-spike) | Explain sudden changes in operational and user metrics | SQL (MySQL), Excel | Email engagement fell from 4,294 to 48 between weeks 34 and 35 |
| 3 | [Hiring Process Analytics](03-hiring-process-analytics) | Understand hiring mix and salary distribution | Excel | 7,164 applicants, 4,693 hired; average offered salary about 49,990 |
| 4 | [IMDB Movie Analysis](04-imdb-movie-analysis) | Factors behind movie ratings | Excel | Duration and budget show a positive relationship with rating |
| 5 | [Bank Loan Case Study](05-bank-loan-case-study) | Identify applicant patterns linked to payment difficulty | Excel, Python (Jupyter) | About 8% of clients had payment difficulties (imbalanced target) |
| 6 | [Impact of Car Features](06-impact-of-car-features) | Optimise pricing and product decisions for a car maker | Excel, Python (Jupyter) | Engine power shows a positive relationship with price |
| 7 | [ABC Call Volume Trend Analysis](07-abc-call-volume-trend) | Call-centre capacity planning | Excel | Peak volume 10 am to 3 pm; staffing plan to cut abandon rate from about 30% to 10% |
